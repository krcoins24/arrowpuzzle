App Permissions

Arrow Escape Quest uses network-only permissions strictly required to load Google AdMob ads and send anonymized Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics reports.

Permissions Requested

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Allows the app to fetch Google AdMob advertisements and send encrypted analytics and crash reports.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Checks whether an internet connection is available before requesting ads or telemetry.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — Included automatically by Google Play Services Ads SDK on Android 13+ for ad measurement and honoring user ad opt-out preferences.

Permissions Never Requested

  • No Camera or Microphone access.
  • No GPS / Precise or Background Location access.
  • No Contacts, Call Logs, or SMS access.
  • No External Storage, Photos, or Media file access.
  • No Calendar, Bluetooth, Health, or Device Sensor access.
