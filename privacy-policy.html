Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Escape Quest (package com.choco.animation.arrow, also referred to as "Arrow Escape", "we", or "the App") handles information when you play our Android puzzle game or visit https://krcoins24.github.io/arrowpuzzle/.

1. Local Storage & No Account Requirement

Arrow Escape Quest does not require account creation or login. We do not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, or payment details. Gameplay progress is stored strictly on your device using Android private SharedPreferences (game_prefs), including your unlocked level (saved_level), per-level stars (stars_level_*), and audio toggle (sound_enabled). System cloud auto-backup is disabled (android:allowBackup="false").

2. Third-Party Services Used

2.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements so the App stays free. AdMob may process your Google Advertising ID (AD_ID), coarse IP-based location, device/OS details, and ad interaction/fraud-prevention signals.

You can opt out of personalized ads or reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy / Google → Ads or via Google Ads Settings. In EEA/UK/Switzerland regions, consent choices are presented before personalized ads load.

2.2 Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) to measure aggregated gameplay trends such as session opens, screen views, Normal Mode / Daily Challenge progression, device model, Android version, and coarse region so we can improve puzzle balance and performance.

2.3 Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to detect crashes and errors. When a crash occurs, Crashlytics collects crash stack traces, a non-personal Crashlytics Installation UUID, OS build, device model, orientation, app version (4.0), and memory/storage state at crash time.

3. How We Use Information

  • Saving local level progress and game preferences on your device.
  • Fixing crashes, freezes, and rendering bugs using Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Understanding aggregate gameplay patterns via Firebase Analytics.
  • Serving advertisements and preventing invalid ad traffic via Google AdMob.
  • Responding to user emails sent voluntarily to our support address.

4. App Permissions (Network Only)

The App requests only network permissions (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) and SDK-managed Advertising ID access (AD_ID). See the Permissions page for full details. We never access Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, SMS, or External Storage.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Links

We do not sell user data. Diagnostic, analytics, and ad-delivery signals are processed by Google LLC under its privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google AdMob Policies

6. Security, Retention & Deletion

All traffic sent to Google AdMob and Firebase is encrypted in transit over HTTPS/TLS. You can delete all local game saves anytime using Settings → Reset Progress inside the App, clearing app storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the App. Analytics and crash data follow standard Google/Firebase retention periods, and any support emails sent to us can be deleted upon request.

7. Children's Privacy

Arrow Escape Quest is intended for a general puzzle audience (ages 13+ or the minimum consent age in your jurisdiction) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians with concerns can email meettejani1000@gmail.com for prompt assistance.

8. Regional Privacy Rights (GDPR / UK / CCPA)

Depending on your region, you have the right to transparency regarding collected data, withdraw ad consent or opt out of personalized advertising, clear local app storage, and request deletion of support correspondence without discrimination.

9. Updates & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves; updates will appear on this page with a revised Effective Date. For privacy questions, contact meettejani1000@gmail.com.
