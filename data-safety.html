Data Safety Summary

Arrow Escape Quest does not collect personal profiles, logins, photos, or contacts. All network communication is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and limited to Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics.

Data Category Service Purpose Storage & Control
Game Progress & Preferences
Unlocked level, stars, sound toggle		 Local SharedPreferences (game_prefs) Save gameplay progress Stored on-device only (allowBackup="false"). Clear anytime via Settings → Reset Progress or uninstall.
App Interactions & Usage
Screen views, session & level stats		 Firebase Analytics Analytics & game balancing Shared with Google/Firebase over HTTPS/TLS for aggregate telemetry.
Crash Logs & Diagnostics
Stack traces, OS version, RAM state		 Firebase Crashlytics Crash & bug fixing Shared with Google/Firebase over HTTPS/TLS strictly for app stability.
Advertising ID & Coarse IP
Google AD_ID, ad views/clicks		 Google AdMob Ad delivery & fraud prevention Encrypted in transit. Opt out or reset ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Security & User Control

  • No Data Selling: We never sell user data to third parties or data brokers.
  • Encrypted Transit: All telemetry and ad traffic sent by Google and Firebase SDKs uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Easy Local Reset: Deleting app data, using Reset Progress in Settings, or uninstalling immediately clears local saves.
