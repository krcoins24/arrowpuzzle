Privacy Policy & Terms for Arrow Escape Quest

Arrow Escape Quest is a brain-teasing directional arrow puzzle game for Android featuring Normal Mode (100 handcrafted levels plus procedural infinite puzzle progression) and Daily Challenge Mode (large 50×50 to 100×100 maze boards with 3 hearts, hints, and zoom/pan controls). This page transparently explains what local game data stays on your device, how our network-only permissions work, and how Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics keep the game free and reliable.

How Your Data Is Handled

We practice data minimization: Arrow Escape Quest does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, or photos. Only minimal diagnostic, analytics, and ad-delivery data is processed through Google & Firebase SDKs over encrypted connections.

1

Local Game Saves

Your unlocked levels, per-level star scores, and sound toggle stay strictly on your phone inside private game_prefs storage with system backup disabled.

2

Google AdMob

Displays ads to keep all 100+ infinite puzzle levels free. AdMob may use the Google Advertising ID and coarse IP region for ad delivery and fraud checks.

3

Firebase Analytics

Measures anonymous gameplay trends such as level starts, completions, and Daily Challenge usage so we can balance difficulty across updates.

4

Firebase Crashlytics

Captures crash stack traces, device OS version, and memory state when a bug occurs so we can fix lag, freezes, or board rendering glitches quickly.

Minimal Access Principle

App Permissions Breakdown

Arrow Escape Quest strictly requests network-only permissions needed for serving Google AdMob advertisements and transmitting anonymized Firebase telemetry.

Used Network & Ad Identifiers

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Connects to Google AdMob servers to fetch ads and sends encrypted Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics reports.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Checks whether Wi-Fi or mobile data is online before loading ads or syncing telemetry so gameplay never stalls needlessly offline.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — Standard Google Play Services Advertising ID permission merged automatically by the Google Mobile Ads / Firebase SDKs on Android 13+ for ad measurement and opt-out respect.

Never Used Sensitive Permissions Blocked

  • No Camera or Microphone access.
  • No Precise GPS Location (no fine/background location permissions).
  • No Contacts, Call Logs, or SMS access.
  • No External Storage, Photos, Media, or Files access outside app-private storage.
  • No Calendar, Health, Bluetooth, or Body Sensors access.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Escape Quest (package identifier com.choco.animation.arrow, also referred to inside the app as "Arrow Escape", "we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you download, install, and play our Android puzzle game via the Google Play Store or visit our official website at https://kcoca772-commits.github.io/.

By installing or using Arrow Escape Quest, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this Policy, please uninstall the App and discontinue its use.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device (No Account Needed)

Arrow Escape Quest does not require account creation, login, passwords, or registration. We do not directly collect your real name, email address, home address, phone number, or payment card details inside the game.

To deliver seamless offline and online puzzle progression, the App stores gameplay preferences strictly on your device using Android private SharedPreferences (game_prefs):

  • Your current unlocked level number (saved_level) across Normal Mode and infinite level progression.
  • Per-level completion star ratings (stars_level_*) and gameplay state such as sound toggle (sound_enabled).

System cloud auto-backup is disabled in the app manifest (android:allowBackup="false"), meaning this local save data never leaves your device through us.

3. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party SDKs

While we do not operate our own user-profile backend servers, Arrow Escape Quest integrates trusted Google services for advertising, anonymized usage analytics, and crash reporting. These third-party SDKs operate over encrypted connections when your device has internet access:

3.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to display in-app advertisements so Arrow Escape Quest can remain free to play. AdMob may automatically collect or process:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID / Ad ID) and device/app identifiers.
  • Approximate / coarse location inferred from IP address (e.g., country or city level, never GPS).
  • Device and network technical signals (device model, Android OS version, screen size, language, mobile carrier/network type).
  • Ad interaction & diagnostic data (ad views, clicks, frequency capping, performance metrics, and invalid-traffic/fraud prevention).
Opting Out of Personalized Ads: On Android, you can opt out of personalized advertising or reset/delete your Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads → Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization, or online at Google Ads Settings. Where required by law (such as the EEA, UK, or Switzerland), we present Google's consent dialog before loading personalized ads.

3.2 Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate player engagement and improve level design (for example, seeing whether Normal Mode or Daily Challenge levels have difficulty spikes). Firebase Analytics may automatically collect:

  • Firebase App Instance ID and basic app lifecycle events (first open, session duration, screen views, and in-game puzzle progression metrics).
  • General device characteristics (Android version, device model, app version 3.0, and coarse country/language setting).

3.3 Firebase Crashlytics (Stability & Bug Diagnostics)

We use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically detect crashes, ANRs (Application Not Responding), and rendering/animation errors. When a crash or non-fatal diagnostic event occurs, Crashlytics collects:

  • Crashlytics Installation UUID (non-personal pseudonymous identifier).
  • Full crash stack traces, exception messages, and active thread state.
  • Device state at the moment of the crash (device model, Android OS build, screen orientation, available RAM/free disk space, and app version).

4. How We Use Collected Information

  • Core Gameplay: Saving your current level, stars earned, and audio preferences locally on your phone.
  • Stability & Performance: Fixing crashes, zoom/pan lag, and level generation bugs via Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Game Improvement: Analyzing anonymous completion rates and session trends via Firebase Analytics.
  • Ad-Supported Access: Serving non-intrusive advertisements and preventing ad fraud via Google AdMob.
  • Legal Compliance & User Support: Responding to support emails sent voluntarily by you and meeting Google Play Store policy obligations.

5. App Permissions (Network Access Only)

Arrow Escape Quest is built on a minimal-permission policy. We only declare network permissions required for Google AdMob ads and Firebase diagnostics/analytics:

  • INTERNET — Allows loading advertisements and transmitting encrypted analytics/crash telemetry.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Allows the App to check network availability before attempting ad or telemetry requests.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — Included by Google Play Services Ads / Analytics SDKs to read or honor your system Advertising ID preferences on Android 13 and higher.

The App does not request access to Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, Phone State, SMS, Call Logs, or External Media/Storage.

6. Data Sharing & Third-Party Policies

We do not sell your personal information to data brokers. Technical and advertising signals described in Section 3 are shared directly with our infrastructure and monetization provider, Google LLC (Google AdMob & Firebase), solely to operate ads, analytics, and crash diagnostics. Please review Google's official policies for further details:

  • How Google uses information from apps using its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob Policies: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

7. Data Security & Encryption

All network transmissions performed by Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics are encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS. Local game data inside game_prefs uses Android sandboxed app-private mode (MODE_PRIVATE) with cloud backup disabled so other apps on your device cannot access it. No internet transmission method is 100% immune to risk, so we minimize the data collected in the first place.

8. Data Retention & Your Deletion Controls

You stay in control of your data at all times:

  • Local Game Saves: Open in-app Settings → Reset Progress to erase saved levels and star ratings immediately, clear App Storage in Android Settings, or uninstall Arrow Escape Quest to delete all local data permanently.
  • Crashlytics & Analytics Data: Retained by Firebase according to standard Google Firebase retention schedules (typically 90 days for Crashlytics logs and 2–14 months for Analytics event data) and automatically purged thereafter.
  • Advertising ID: Reset or delete your Advertising ID anytime via Android system settings.
  • Support Emails: If you email meettejani1000@gmail.com for help, you may request deletion of your email correspondence at any time.
