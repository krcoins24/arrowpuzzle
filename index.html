Arrow Escape Quest

Arrow Escape Quest is an Android directional arrow maze puzzle game featuring Normal Mode (100 core levels plus infinite level generation) and Daily Challenge Mode (50×50 to 100×100 grids) with 3 hearts, hints, and zoom/pan controls.

Read Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions Google Play

Quick App & Compliance Summary

  • Account Required: None. No login, name, email, or phone number required to play.
  • Game Saves: Unlocked levels, stars, and sound settings are stored locally on your device (game_prefs).
  • Permissions: Network access only (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, plus Google Play AD_ID).
  • Third-Party SDKs: Google AdMob (ads), Firebase Analytics (usage metrics), and Firebase Crashlytics (crash diagnostics).
